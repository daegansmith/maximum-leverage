Interviews

Conversations with the people who taught me, and the people I taught.

I never planned any of these.

I'd meet someone doing something I couldn't stop thinking about, and I'd ask. “Would you mind if I interviewed you?”

In all those years, one person said no.

Each one comes two ways: a short lesson with the best idea in it, and the full conversation if you want to dig in.
