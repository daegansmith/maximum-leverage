Privacy Policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

This policy explains what personal information I collect through this website and my courses, how I use it, and the choices you have. This site is run by Daegan Smith, Washington, DC.

What I collect

  • When you buy: your name, email address and billing details. Payment is handled by Stripe, which processes your card. I receive confirmation of the payment and limited details such as your name, email and the last four digits of your card. I never see or store your full card number.
  • When you join my email list: your name and email address.
  • When you email me: whatever you choose to include in your message.

Browsing this website

This site doesn't use advertising cookies or tracking tools. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which may record standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type for security and operations, under GitHub's own privacy policy.

How I use your information

  • To deliver what you bought and schedule any consultations.
  • To send emails you signed up for. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing takes effect right away.
  • To answer your questions and provide support.
  • To keep records required for tax and accounting.

Who I share it with

I don't sell or rent your personal information. I share it only with the services needed to run the business: Stripe for payments, my email service provider for sending email, and Google for email correspondence. Each handles your information under its own privacy policy. I may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long I keep it

I keep purchase records as long as tax and accounting rules require. You can ask me to remove you from my email list at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information I hold about you by emailing reply@maximum-leverage.com. I'll respond within 30 days.

Children

My courses are for adults. I don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If I change this policy, I'll post the new version here with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: reply@maximum-leverage.com.
