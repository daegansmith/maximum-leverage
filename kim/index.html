Her Journey

Adrianne has dedicated her life to the practice and teaching of yoga. Beginning in Hawaii, she discovered yoga as a way to explore curiosity and connect deeply with herself and others. Her journey led her through intensive trainings, including time at Sattva Yoga Academy in Rishikesh, India, where she deepened her understanding of yoga philosophy and practice.

As a CorePower Yoga Master Trainer, she taught hundreds of classes and trained teachers across multiple studios. Today, she teaches at some of DC's finest yoga studios and leads private sessions, corporate wellness programs, and retreats. Her students come back not just for her skill, but for the way she holds space—present, curious, and completely dedicated to their practice.
