The Practice

Adrianne teaches yoga as exploration and discovery. Her classes are rooted in traditional practice, informed by years of training and study, and brought to life through her genuine presence with each student.

She has studied extensively, including time at Sattva Yoga Academy in Rishikesh, India. As a former CorePower Master Trainer, she brought yoga to hundreds of practitioners. Now based in DC, she teaches at the city's finest studios and leads private sessions, corporate retreats, and transformative yoga journeys.
