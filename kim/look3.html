Yoga is the journey of the self, through the self, to the self. Adrianne teaches this journey—not as a series of poses, but as a living practice of presence and discovery.

Her classes are rooted in tradition, informed by intensive study including time at Sattva Yoga Academy in Rishikesh, India, and brought to life through genuine presence with each student. She has trained teachers, led corporate retreats, and guided practitioners from all backgrounds.
