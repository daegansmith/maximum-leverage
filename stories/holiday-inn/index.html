The Holiday Inn In Bethesda

Subject: How did I get here?

As I sat in the last seat of the Bethesda, Maryland Holiday Inn, careful not to be seen by anyone I knew, I could almost smell the desperation.

There I was.

One of about 400 people who had somehow found their way to this "meeting." I wondered if they had all seen the same commercial I had a few days earlier.

I had come home from my job as a traveling sales rep in the Washington, DC area after a three-hour stint in the gym. (It was really all I had to look forward to back then.)

I remember jumping in the shower and feeling something I had never felt before in my life.

My heart quickened.

My breath grew shallow.

I could feel my eyes going dark.

As I reached for the shower head to stop myself from falling, there, at that moment...

I felt true horror.

My mind wandered to my daily routine, and I realized, for the first time, that...

Every day of my adult life I had done the exact same thing.

Wake up at 6:31 a.m. sharp. Hit the snooze button exactly 11 times before actually getting out of bed at 7:50 or so.

Always so tired.

Stumble to the shower like a zombie. Clean up, put on my suit, and leave to be a paid commercial for some company.

Every day, this is what I did...

The fear came on sharp with one simple question...

"If this is what life is like now, what's it going to be like 10 years from now?"

The horror took over with the answer.

"Nothing."

That moment changed me forever.

I had "woken up" to the painful reality that life was NOT going the way I had planned as a young kid who once dreamed of owning his own orange Lamborghini.

Now, as an adult, all I could see, if...

I stayed on the path set before me, was mediocrity.

Settling.

I couldn't let that future come true, but what do you do?

"How DO you get out of this damn job?"

"How DO you free yourself?"

That night, after watching my favorite movie, Apocalypse Now, on my couch with a couple of Sierra Nevadas, I saw something I'd never seen in something I'd seen many, many times before.

There, in that commercial promising wealth through real estate, where I had only ever seen scam artists and charlatans, I saw, for the first time, "hope."

The hope of a chance to change.

As I sat in the back of that Holiday Inn conference room, I teetered between complete embarrassment and regret for seeing opportunity in a real estate infomercial a few days earlier.

What came after that night, I could never have imagined.

From my little laptop, I've generated millions of dollars, literally at the push of a button.

A reality where, any time I chose to write a five-minute message and send it to my list of 180,000 email subscribers around the world, between $2,000 and $12,000, on average, came back within 24 hours.

That reality let me travel to places I never would have imagined.

Can you believe that before my embarrassing night at that Holiday Inn, I didn't even have a passport?

I went on to see Singapore, Malaysia, Japan, Nicaragua, El Salvador and the Greek Isles, and then, on a whim...

I moved to Maui. I leased a house in Wailea with an elevator and a saltwater pool, less than an eight-minute walk from the MOST beautiful beaches in the world, and my daily reality was literally...

I'd wake up in the morning, sit on my lanai, and watch whales from my back porch.

On a whim, from Washington, DC to Maui, Hawaii.

Within a week, I made the decision and I was there.

Forget about that little voice that asks questions like...

"What about your homes in Washington, DC?"

That voice? Silenced. Ignored.

I just left them for a year-long extended vacation without a thought about "if" I could afford it or if it made any sense.

I wanted to go, so I simply went.

Imagine being able to make decisions that way.

Well, you can...

You can make decisions like that any time you want when you can do this:

Create income with leverage, on demand.

That IS the ultimate skill. Knowing how to do that IS freedom.

Here's what I mean...

Right before I sat down to write this, I sent out an email. When I got up to get a glass of water, I saw I had already made $1,750 while writing to you, and I wasn't surprised.

That's normal.

That's how it always happened, so that's simply how it's supposed to happen.

Now...

A normal person would never believe this, but it was my reality, and it can be yours.

Sitting in that back row of the Holiday Inn changed my life for one reason.

One person, really. But before I met that person, I witnessed one of the most important lessons I've ever learned...

Right before my eyes, I saw just how quickly anyone's fortune can change.

Before the man who would change my life ever spoke, another man shared his story from the stage.

Hair slicked back, black suit, a smile on his face, he told the audience to get out of their seats and run to the back of the room to buy his real estate system, and my jaw dropped.

I counted.

Well over 100 people waited patiently to hand this man their credit cards and be charged $997 for his course.

"You mean, in one hour, he just made three times more than I make in a year?"

It would have taken me 10 years of perfect corporate butt-kissing to get a salary anywhere close to what this guy just pulled down in an hour, right before my eyes.

It was almost magic.

There, even before I met the man who would change my life, I learned a lesson I want to share with you.

In fact, this might be the MOST important lesson you CAN learn.

"He (or she) who sells well and has an audience is already wealthy."

That sounds simple and obvious, but I didn't really "get" it until it came to me as a question...

If you can find your own way to answer this question and live by it, it will change your financial destiny forever. I guarantee it.

The question...

"How can I fall in love with selling?"

Find the answer to that, and the only place it can lead is a life of infinite freedom and uncontrollable wealth. But I'm getting ahead of myself.

The "pro" who had just beaten three years of my income in an hour walked off stage, and although I was amazed, his solution wasn't for me.

I "almost" felt like the evening was a complete waste.

Then this short, fat, bald little man stepped on stage.

God bless that man. He doesn't know it, but he changed my life.

When HE stepped on stage, here's what happened.

I remember...

My pupils dilated. My eyes widened. My breath slowed. My ears could hear nothing but his words as he shared...

That the internet was a place where regular people were making a full-time living.

A mental revolution took place inside my skull.

I was more than intrigued.

Until then, the ONLY thing the internet was to me was a place to do research and email friends.

When he started showing pictures of the simple little sites he and others like him had made, sites making $1,000 a month passively, I simply couldn't contain my excitement.

I remember clenching my jaw. I rocked back and forth in my chair. I couldn't stop my hands from jittering. My path. I had found it...

I believe we only find a few great obsessions in life, and I "knew" this one was mine.

God bless that short, fat, bald man.

If not for him, the journey that led me to writing to you would never have begun.

I bought his thing (it was a piece of crap, but who cares), went straight home, and started researching "how" people were making passive and residual income online.

Curiosity is one of the MOST powerful forces in the world.

My curiosity changed me from a timid kid who couldn't speak in front of an audience into a guy who, on a whim and completely out of curiosity, decided to see "if" people would come to a seminar if I put one on.

Not only did they come, but with three weeks' notice, 100 of them showed up from all corners of the world, each paying me a measly $197 to be there.

Two days, I stood before that audience. Two days, I spoke and did things that would have crippled me with fear just a few years earlier, and I LOVED every moment of it.

When all was said and done, that weekend created, let's just say, at least six figures.

Not bad for two days.

How ironic.

I used to look at the people on stage at seminars like that as charlatans, and the audience as desperate. Now that I've played both positions, I see it completely differently.

Seminars are places of great change.

They are places of personal transformation.

If you LOVE something, or you're even slightly curious about it, you owe it to yourself to immerse yourself in that subject. In a room filled with people just like you, you will change for the better.

If there's one lesson I can give you here, it's simply this...

Go to more seminars.

Back to the story.

Realize...

We just jumped from the back of the room to the front of the room, loving it.

"What happened in between?"

I'll tell you this much for now...

A lot happened.

A lot of growth. A lot of sacrifice. A lot of risk. A lot of hope. A lot of trust. A lot of mistakes. A lot of money made. A lot of money spent. A lot of money wasted. A lot of focus. A lot of confusion. But mostly, a lot of curiosity.

"I wonder what it would feel like to be financially free?"

That question guided me to my freedom. So I ask you, what's yours?

To the top,

Daegan
