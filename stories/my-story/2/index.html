Something Was Missing

Man.

You know...

I can't quite put my finger on it, but ALL I can remember now is feeling like something was missing.

Like, this isn't ALL it could be about, could it?

I had just graduated college.

Duke University.

I had wrestled all four years even though half the time I hated it.

Nothing worse in life than doing something you once loved with every fiber of your being, and remembering what that felt like, but... no longer feeling that way about that activity.

It must be what purgatory, or the higher circles of hell, feel like.

That's what wrestling was to me.

I wasn't bad.

I was ranked nationally.

I placed 2nd in ACCs my senior year, but the LOVE... It had left me LONG ago.

After college, with my new B.S. in Biology and a Minor in Chemistry, I hit the working world.

It took about a year of being a bike courier making a cool $316.00 every two weeks, and almost getting hit by several cars, and...

Getting fired for sleeping on the job as a lifeguard at the most, let's call it...

"Flamboyant" hotel here in DC.

But then I landed me a good job.

Parents... proud.

Your boy Daegan entered the corporate world as a Pharmaceutical sales rep for Merck.

$36,000 a year and a company car.

I thought I had it made.

It was an absolute BLAST my first few months, but...

That "new" excitement wore thin quickly.

Thinking back on it now, I really think the only reason that I'd go to the gym every day was just to see if I could bench press 405lbs, and then getting 465lbs was really...

Out of boredom.

My job amounted to me being nothing more than a walking, talking commercial that no one wanted to watch.

Outwardly, as I walked into doctors' offices, sure, I smiled, but...

Deep down inside I felt meaningless. Worthless.

And then, the one morning in the shower, when I really thought about the grand scope of it all.

I felt a sharp sting of intense and pure terror pondering this as what the rest of existence would be.

Something was missing...

Meaning.

Significance.

I had more to give the world than this, but... What?

I didn't know, but I did know that THIS... Going to see people every day who didn't want to see me, and didn't care who I was, that existence.....

It had an expiration date.

I think, like most of us, at that point, I started searching.

Searching for some meaning.

Some ray of hope.

What I found was an infomercial.

We all do things we later regret, and...

Picking up the phone to reserve my seat for that meeting after watching that infomercial was one of those for me.

The moment I walked into that room...

That room filled with some 400 or so random - and hopelessly lost in the search for meaning in their lives - individuals, I regretted it.

I remember looking into a few of their eyes and seeing it.

Seeing the desperation and fear that was so thick that I could almost, almost... smell it.

Then, moments later, the feeling of intense panic flooded into me.

The moment I realized...

"I was one of them."

More embarrassed than I have the vocabulary to describe, I sat in the last row, careful not to look up or be noticed.

Then something happened that I never expected.

A man in a sharp suit got on stage and started speaking.

I just remember feeling compelled. Sucked into the drama he shared.

Time didn't exist for a few moments there, and then...

At the end, he did something that I had never seen in my entire life.

From this room of panicked and desperate souls, he pulled over $100,000 out of their pockets in just over an hour.

Magic.

I had no idea WHAT it was that he did. I do now, and we'll get to it, but I knew...

It was different from anything I had ever known.

Haha.

The irony of it all now is pretty compelling - you'll see what I mean soon enough.

I had learned something about this one thing before I even knew what it was that day.

If you have the right audience, you can NEVER go broke.

Ahh...

I'm getting ahead of myself.

No worries, we'll come back, but let's keep time warping a bit...

Little did I know that about a year after that day, what I discovered in that room would lead me to a position where I was NOT only dangerously in debt, but...

I had put myself in a position where I was about to lose the most important thing in the world to me at that point.

Why?

We'll know sooner than you think, and... much more.

For now, just remember...

If you have the right audience, you can never go _____.

I'll pick up where I left off in the next chapter.

Daegan
