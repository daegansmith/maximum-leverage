My Story · Chapter 6 of 7

The Source, Revealed

As you read this penultimate chapter just remember this phrase...

"No matter how many times you get knocked down, if you keep getting up, nothing can defeat you."

Today, I reveal the source.

The wellspring of freedom, power, wealth, and abundance.

THE ultimate asset.

Once you have this one thing you need nothing else.

I only wish I had realized this sooner myself.

There I was, partner gone, left with a system with no programmer and support for when things go bad.

$34,000 stolen because somehow "the check must have gotten lost in the mail."

Bank accounts drained.

Only to find out that I never had a partner in the first place.

I should have shared this in the last chapter as a second lesson - Never get into a partnership unless there's no other option and life depends on it.

Man...

If it hadn't been for that little Free CD that I put together, I'd have been shit out of luck once again.

Thank god I had.

That CD saved my business.

At some point the year prior, probably on some subconscious level sensing my fate, I had decided to take the recording of a seminar presentation I had done at the "Putting The Net In Network Marketing" seminar a few years back, where I shared the exact steps I used to build my first 8,000+ person team, on CD and offer it for free + shipping that led into a CD of the month club that I had put together.

That little "Recruit Like Crazy" CD turned out to be THE thing (with one other ingredient) that created my first $1M year.

Here's the funny thing...

Prior to putting together that sales letter and offer, I had been giving that exact same content away for free as an MP3 online, which highlights an important lesson.

The value your potential customers perceive is the value you give them to perceive.

The exact same thing that you give away for free today could, if positioned properly, be sold for whatever price you so choose. The power really is in your hands.

When my partner left I simply got to work.

I turned that free CD into the highest converting offer that I had ever created (which my now friend, Russell Brunson, let's say "modeled" for his DotComSecrets free DVD sales letter. As they say, modeling is the best form of flattery, right?)

I split tested every element of that page I could.

I added bonus after bonus, to the point where, if you saw it and didn't spend $6.97 to get it, you were either a fool or you didn't speak English.

It took me about 18 months to get there, but seeing $3,000 - $4,000 show up in your bank account every day was worth it.

I only wish I had paid attention earlier.

The exact same mistake that caused my first "opportunity" to close its doors killed my first $1M business.

I had no idea when it happened. I was blindsided by the email.

When my merchant account sent me the email that said they no longer wanted to do business because my merchant stats were too risky for them, it was already too late.

There was nothing I could do.

I tried every trick in the book to "improve" my metrics, but...

It came down to the simple fact that I had created an offer that converted too well, and too many of the wrong people bought it and then did chargebacks.

The day my merchant account was closed and the $1M business I had built simply wasn't there anymore was one of the hardest business experiences I've ever had.

For 4 months I simply did nothing in my depression. I almost invited financial chaos.

I felt like, after ALL I had been through, I didn't deserve this. I wanted to crawl in a hole and die.

When I finally did come around, and it wasn't easy... I decided that if I was going to give it my all one last time, I would NEVER make the mistake I had just made.

Instead of creating something that sold to the lowest common denominator, I very purposefully launched my Maximum Leverage business the opposite way.

Instead of selling "free" I made this membership $97 as a trial and then $197 per month (the most expensive membership in my market).

I had no idea what would happen, but I wasn't losing another merchant account, and I wasn't attracting deadbeat customers ever again.

What DID happen taught me a lesson I will never forget.

Within 5 months, I was back at $128,000 per month.

Almost 1/4 of the time it took to get there the first time, and my customers, well...

You see them out there every day.

The guys that were members of Maximum Leverage are now the guys doing $50,000 to several million dollars per month online.

Remember this one - You ALWAYS have the power to choose who your customers will be , and a business filled with bad customers or the wrong customers is not a business worth building.

Maximum Leverage not only re-ignited my passion, it was SO much fun.

I moved to Maui. Lived in paradise, and I thought I had it ALL figured out this time. Haha...

I couldn't have been more wrong.

In Maui, I found my depression ALL over again. This time though, I was in paradise.

Another harsh lesson that I couldn't have anticipated.

Within a span of two months ALL of my major traffic sources were shut down. I had no control over it. It happened to everyone.

This time though, I had no answer.

From millions to literally zero coming in. With only $30,000 in the bank, saved from the last month of business, and over $30,000 in monthly expenses staring at me.....

I had a decision to make...

Try one last-ditch effort, and if it works - survive (maybe).

If it goes wrong, I've got nothing left.

Before I could make that decision, I had to leave paradise.

There I was...

I had reached "the dream."

I had fallen so many times only to get back up.

I had literally given everything I had to this journey.

What hurt the most was, I had brought Kim, my wife, with me to Maui.

I had asked her to quit her job, and she trusted me.

Now...

I couldn't even look her in the eyes.

Our house in Maui leased from April 10th to April 10th, and we closed the house and headed home.

I arrive back home in DC and have maybe three days before IT happens......

And things go from bad to worse...

Right around April 15th, I check my mailbox, and in it is a letter from the IRS saying that I owed them almost $983,000 in back taxes, and that I had to pay them within 2 weeks or I could potentially be put in jail.

That one was the hardest to take.

The blow that hits you while you're down.

What was I going to do? I had to do something.

I guess if I could tell you WHY I did what I did then.....

The only answer I could give you was - I felt like I had nothing to lose.

It was over anyway.

Once again, I tapped the source, a certain segment that had been sleeping, that I hadn't paid much attention to.

Within two weeks of doing so I had generated almost $140,000 in revenue.

It wasn't until then, when I was at my lowest point, that I saw it, the source, for what it truly was...

The ultimate asset.

In every instance where my business had failed, this was the IT which had resurrected it.

The list.

If you build a list of subscribers who know, love and trust you - you can never go out of business.

No matter what, it ALWAYS produces profits, and rights all wrongs IF you build it right. If you treat it right.

It will take care of you for a lifetime.

What I didn't get, for a long time, was that it's not just who's on the list. It's what you do with the list that determines your success.

Daegan
