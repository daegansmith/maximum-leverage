I Almost Couldn't Believe It Happened To Me

You know you dream about it.

You do crazy stuff like chant and scream and visualize it, but when it happens...

It's almost too much to believe.

For me, I remember it like it was yesterday...

I found my focus and that changed everything, and just in the nick of time. You'll see what I mean in this chapter.

But, what was that focus?

At my lowest point, I was one of those network marketer wanna-be's. The type of guy the industry gets rich off of.

The guy that buys all the "red hot" leads at $5 to $15 a piece.

The guy that practices his phone sales script night and day.

The guy that goes broke while all those doling out that "priceless" expert advice get rich with every recommendation they make to him.

Thousands of leads. Purchased. Dialed. Then, swiftly rejected by.

That was me.

I didn't close a single sale.

In fact, the only sale I did "close" was one day when I got frustrated and blasted out an email to ALL of them.

My one thing.

I wanted to build the dream business network marketing "promised" but without the phone. I couldn't stand that thing at that point, so...

I set my mind to doing just that. It didn't matter that I didn't know anything.

I was smart enough. I had (have) a brain, so... Once I knew what I wanted to do, I simply started to do my research.

For the first time ever, instead of buying a course that promised to solve all my problems, I pieced the solution together myself.

I figured that, if I could figure out what successful online businesses did, then I could see ALL the things they ALL had in common, and...

Whatever THAT was.

That was exactly what I needed to have too.

It took me about a month, but I had compiled my research, and constructed my list, and hypothesis.

"If I could remove myself from selling, and find or create an online system that could do it for me, then... I'd have a much better chance to succeed."

And if that were true, then I took my logical argument one step further and defined the one thing I would have to do.

If I had a system that would do the selling for me, then the ONE thing I would have to do would be control the quality and flow of leads that I would put into that system, and...

THAT would be the determining factor of my success.

Now that I knew what to look for, when it happened across my computer screen in the signature file of a Malaysian's post in the MLM.com forum - I knew what it was when I saw it.

I knew it immediately. Joined, almost on the spot.

Then, I got to my task.

The getting of good leads.

What I ultimately decided on was to do newsletter marketing, to buy a few "solo ads" - it wasn't just the fastest way to get good leads, but if I chose wisely I'd find higher quality prospects than I could find anywhere else.

Then BOOM!

I get off of work one day.

Arrive home. My ad had gone out that day, and I see 77 new leads and 11 sign ups.

Months of painful rejection and no recruits, and then I spend a few dollars on a little newsletter ad and I recruit 11 people? Where were they before?

You almost have to laugh at the irony of it all, but along with that comes one of THE most important lessons I can ever teach you.

In most cases, the path to success is NOT the path to mastery. It's NOT about getting better.

I was FAR from being the best sales guy on the phone, and this left me with two options.

1. Go broke trying to get good.

2. Remove myself from that role and replace me with something that would convert.

The ONLY thing stopping you from getting your goals is figuring out what you're trying to master that you have no business or reason to, and figuring out how to replace yourself with something that can do a far better job.

In other words, success is more about removing constraints and simplifying than it is about "getting good," and that's EXACTLY why the point at hand, our overall topic, will change your life. Why? I'll get to it.

For now, let's get back to the story...

I had that first small success and you better believe I pushed that "easy" button over and over again.

Within a few months, I had a team of hundreds. Me... some no-name nobody, and there I am - doing the damn thing, but...

Be careful what you ask for.

Before long, I had ALL kinds of people calling me day and night.

Some of them were prospects wanting details before joining, others were overly needy and non-self-reliant team members who created the belief for themselves that if they had my "magic" (or whatever they thought) ALL their dreams would come true.

Back then, I was WAY nicer than I am today. I DO NOT put up with that these days, and...

That wasn't my only problem.

I was making money, but I had hit my capacity for how much traffic I could get with my personal budget, AND I knew if I could only grow it, my little biz would grow much faster.

And that's where the hammer dropped.

In the car, on a ride along, with my boss Clarence...

It was one of those awkward "ride alongs" where we both sat there silently because we both knew what was about to happen.

That only put MORE pressure on me.

I was making money, but not enough yet to support my life.

On that car ride home pondering the inevitable, that's where I found the solution.

I had learned a few months prior that the best way to get rid of negative emotions and thoughts is to replace them, so I put one of my favorite personal development CDs in the Ford Taurus's CD player, and that's where I heard one word that took my team from a few hundred to over 8,000...

Co-op.

I didn't know exactly what to do, but I knew I had to test this idea.

I went home, emailed my team, and told them we were going to start a team co-op.

The next morning I had over $1,000 in my Paypal account.

That first month, I collected over $3,200.

The next month, over $5,000. Then $8,000. Then it topped off around $11,000 or so per month.

Needless to say, my team exploded, and...

When I was "made redundant," as they say in the UK - it didn't matter.

And, funny thing is... A few months later, my former boss joined me in my little business.

I had tapped into the source, and I didn't even know it.

It was an amazing time in my life.

Right until the moment that it wasn't.

I still remember the day the company announced it was closing.

All that belief building, and "we're in this till the end together" talk. Yeah... The end meaning when you decided for it all to end.

All my hard work - gone.

I would have been a dead man floating if not for one thing...

And in the next chapter... you'll know that one thing.

You'll have it once I explain it.

For now, remember this...

Lesson #3 - The far more certain path to success is NOT the path of mastery. It's the path of removing the constraints that are in the way.

Mine was my inability to sell.

What's yours? Find it, then remove it, and you won't believe how close you've always been to exactly where you wanted to go.

P.S. I should have paid attention to WHY the company closed its doors. It would have saved me ALL kinds of headache. I guess, most lessons, I gotta learn the hard way so you don't have to.

In the next chapter, when you learn this one, DO NOT EVER forget it. It would have been worth its weight in gold for me to know, and will save your business.

Daegan
