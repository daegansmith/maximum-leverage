Fiercely Addicted And On The Brink Of Oblivion

Remember, in the last chapter, I shared who I was and where I was before I found my life's passion.

How I couldn't quite put my finger on what it was, but...

I had this feeling of being insignificant. Worthless. As if my existence meant nothing and would never mean anything.

That feeling led me to a hotel seminar where I witnessed magic.

A man, dressed in a sharp suit, captivating an audience, and within 90 minutes generating over $100,000 for it.

I had no idea how or what "it" was that he had done, but that experience changed me.

It opened me up to the possibility of something new.

That something new would, within a year, leave me with a fierce addiction that almost destroyed my life.

How did I get there? Let me tell you...

Like I said, there was something about the feat I had just witnessed. It left me in a state. A haze of mystical wonderment. Like there was magic in this world that I knew nothing of.

Then, a man stepped on stage that, today... I wish I could track down.

This man. Through his passion alone. Changed my life.

He shared this thing called the internet. He shared how regular folks could actually make money on it.

So convincing was his passion that it birthed a new curiosity in me.

A curiosity that soon turned into an obsession.

An obsession that led to addiction, and... the bringer of my complete destruction.

I've told you some of this before, but as soon as I heard his words, I couldn't get them out of my head.

"If you just stare at the computer long enough, money will start to pour out."

Maybe, or maybe something else.

Something a LOT worse.

As soon as I heard him speak I couldn't wait to get home and start searching the internet for how to make money online.

You know how they say it's not the first hit of heroin that does you in, it's the second?

For me, that's exactly what opening my mind to the internet was like.

My first hit was spending $40 for this old guy's "website deal" at that event.

My second, my first eBook purchase.

That's all it took.

Before long, it was ALL I could think about.

I bought anything I could.

Sure, the first purchases were small.

$67 here. $97 there, but after I got a little practice in me...

If I saw a course on how to make this magic box make me rich and it looked good, I couldn't stop thinking about it until I had it.

I was ravenous. Reckless.

Spending weeks chasing schemes in traffic exchanges.

Spending $1000s on things that were clearly scams.

Remember when I said I'd share a few brutally honest truths with you?

Here's one...

One course that I purchased was called "The Simplest Way To Make $1M Online."

It wasn't that expensive, but then... There wasn't much to it.

Maybe 11 pages.

Inside, this course explained exactly how to do exactly what the title explained.

It shared how anyone could go to insane asylums, homeless shelters, or elderly care facilities and find someone who was likely to die, and how you could then take out an insurance policy on them, and cash in when they died.

I was sooo deep in this addiction to "make it work" that I almost did it.

I'm embarrassed and ashamed that I ever entertained them, but I share this with you so you can see me.

I'm no different, no better, and in reality, I probably have deficiencies that most people will never have to deal with.

Yet I'm here.

I've created multimillionaires and I'm dedicated to the cause of financial independence, and to you.

While I was "entertaining" doing the immoral and unethical, on the flipside, my regular life...

It was in shambles.

Somewhere halfway into my descent into madness I destroyed my relationship with my father.

My girlfriend of two years, who, now that I think back, really did want to get married, I ignored.

My job. It didn't matter either, and...

My boss, he knew, and I knew he knew. It was only a matter of time.

Credit card debt? Yeah.

I was literally hanging on by a thread.

And, amongst it all, I did have a few successes, but nothing stable.

I had even stumbled on the source, the focus of these here lessons, and created money almost effortlessly by it, but...

I didn't even realize it.

So blinded by the search for instant riches, that I didn't even notice I had already stumbled onto it. Nope.

In fact, I wouldn't know that for another 8 months or so.

First, I had to lose it all.

Broke. Juggling one credit card for another. All I had was my madness.

Laura, she left.

Friends? Now I was the weird guy who never went out anymore.

Family, embarrassed of me.

The job, I had no idea how close I was to the end.

If it hadn't been for one thing...

I wouldn't be here to tell you this story.

I happened onto a guy's list by the name of Dave Valleries, and there was something different about him.

He seemed real.

I read ALL his emails, and... One of them proved to be my savior.

It was the one where he released his new product (which I still have) - "You Can Make A Living Online."

It was $497.

I had just enough room on my credit cards for it.

That course. It changed everything for me.

Not even the course itself. Really... Just one or two paragraphs. Where he said...

"You know, all the people that are still searching for success tend to look at the online world with a buffet mentality. Just try some of everything and one of them is sure to work, but...

When I look at my own business, and others who I know are successful, they do the opposite.

They focus on ONE thing.

Because they do, they then have the power of leverage on their side.

Instead of spreading themselves across 1,000 different projects, they focus on one and put ALL their effort and energy into it.

They specialize. They deliver one specific type of value to one specific marketplace, and because of this, their customers know who they are and what they offer, and do business with them."

As soon as I read that, I got it.

I remember putting the book down and walking over to my window and thinking "What's my one thing?"

As soon as I found it, it seemed as if it wasn't long before success found me.

2+ years of misery and madness, and within two months of reading that course, and asking myself that one specific question, I was making thousands of dollars per month.

Not long after, I had hit the six-figure per year mark, and then... Much larger figures.

What's your one thing going to be?

Think about it. That one little idea could become a million-dollar business. (Believe me, stranger things have happened.)

Again... I'm getting ahead of myself.

And the truth is, it wasn't smooth sailing. Far from it...

All will be revealed soon.

Lesson #1 - If you have the right audience, you can never go ____.

Lesson #2 - This question "What's your one thing?"
