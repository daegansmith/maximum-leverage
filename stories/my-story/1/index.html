My Story · Chapter 1 of 7

It Begins

What's this story really about? If you know me, I'm not one to waste your time, so...

We'll be covering THE most important topic of them all. One that... if you pay attention, will transform your life.

At heart, I've always been something of a philosopher and a scientist, and the question that I've pondered the most over the years is...

What is the ONE truth?

The one thing that, once one realized its inherent power, its truth, immediately opens to that person a world of immense power and immediate dynamic change?

Behind this question is this:

What is it that we all yearn for in our lives? The one universal thread that ties us all together, and...

Over the years I've had the opportunity to speak with and interact with MANY aspiring entrepreneurs. Some, I've had the rare fortune to play a hand in transforming into multimillionaires.

You know what I've realized about ALL of us?

We ALL wake up in the morning optimistic about our future.

We all believe that a better life...

More joy.

More freedom.

More power to define our path.

More abundance.

Is waiting for us, just over the horizon. It's true.

And so, in realizing this, I also realized something else...

Most of us attempt to live the fullest life that we possibly can by focusing on one of the forces for change I just shared.

About a year ago I started to wonder about this...

Surely seeking more joy, freedom, power, or abundance will grant us a fuller life if sought directly, but...

Where do they come from?

That is, is there a source? Is there a wellspring for them all?

See... It's great to seek out and find joy or freedom, but as with all things in life, those ends in and of themselves tend to be fleeting.

Here one day. Maybe not here the next, but...

If you could find the source for creating any or all of these great rewards, then what MUST follow is that you will then have tapped into an infinite source for them all.

I'd rather have that.

The ability to create all that I want in life, when I choose to will it into existence, above anything else.

If only I could find that, then NOT only would I be changed, but I'd have the greatest ability to change for the better, and that...

Is exactly what I'm going to share with you in these chapters.

The source of it all.

Just imagine.

What it would be like to experience the true meaning of abundance on all levels.

Anything you need... you can have.

Better said, you can create at will.

If that were the case, what would you create for yourself?

For those closest to you?

How would you contribute? What would you give back?

For now, sit with that thought. In the next chapter, our journey begins.

I'll see you then.

Daegan
