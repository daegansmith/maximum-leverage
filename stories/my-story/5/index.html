What Happened After It Was All Taken Away

It felt like, just as quickly as I had gained some momentum and success, it was taken away.

What ate at me the most was the fact that ultimately I had no control and say in what happened to the income stream, the asset I had created.

It was just gone.

The lesson I pulled from that experience was, NEVER let any other business control your cash flow.

In your business, you MUST have your central hub be money that gets to YOU and put in YOUR bank account, or else you ultimately have nothing.

If it wasn't for the fact that I had created my first product by the time they closed the doors - I would have had absolutely nothing to show for ALL the blood, sweat and tears.

Lucky for me, or better said... We all create our own luck.

I'm fortunate that I NOT only had my own info product that was making me at least $6,000 per month, and...

I had taken the step to invest the $30,000 I had saved from my earnings into taking my products and leveraging them into a fully functional generic prospecting system.

If you've ever seen my buddy Brian Fanale's business - MyLeadSystemPro - then you know what I'm talking about.

I'm old.

My system was called "Power Prospecting System" and in those days it was one of the big 3 that were out there, and it's directly where my students like Raymond Fong and Fernando Ceballos and Jonathan Budd sprang from.

They were all members of my prospecting system.

Investing in creating that system was one of the best decisions I could have made at that point, and simultaneously, one of the worst.

Let me share the good before the bad...

Because this system basically took my products that were already converting, and allowed affiliates to promote them and get paid for their efforts, the $6,000 that I would have made on my own just doing my own advertising ballooned into over $30,000 most months.

And the even more powerful benefit that I still remember to this day was that because I had built a system and a community of individuals inside of that system, the relationship I had with my members was extremely strong.

When my first company went under, after a few months I decided to find a new one.

(BTW - this sequence has NOTHING to do with network marketing, so don't ever take anything I'm sharing as "this is for those guys" - This is pure direct response. All principles and lessons can be leveraged in any industry.)

When I did, I realized the power that was behind the community I had created.

One email to introduce this new company turned into $34,000 in 3 days.

I was blown away.

Everything would have been great if it were NOT for the mistake I made out of sheer ignorance.

See, when I started "Power Prospecting System" I had a discussion with a programmer of the system.

A guy who became my partner [named in the original, S03189], and when we spoke he shared that he was impressed with what I was bringing to the table.

That led to him sharing that he thought it would be a good idea for us to partner on the system as a project.

I don't know what it was, maybe I didn't know any better, but I felt humbled by his offer and accepted.

One of the worst decisions I've ever made in my life.

I'm left to basically do everything in the business, but I think, no...

"He's a good guy. He'll contribute more over time."

Fast forward to the day I send that email about the new biz that does $34,000. Prior to that, my partner and I agree to join as a "team" - I still trust him.

I think nothing of the fact that all checks are set to go to him.

To make a long story short, I never saw a dime of that money because "The check must have gotten lost in the mail."

Later, I find out that in setting up the LLC (which I let him do) - the entire company is solely under my name.

We never were partners on paper, and one day, he absconds to Sweden.

Drains the bank accounts, and leaves me with the entire tax burden.

I share this so you know, so you see - the me. My path. My journey.

But, like the phoenix, I found a way to rise from my grave once more.

The next chapter is the MOST important lesson of them all.

They've ALL been building to this...

The "source" will be revealed.

For now, don't forget the lesson - The core of your business MUST be money YOU make directly or you're not in business.

Daegan
