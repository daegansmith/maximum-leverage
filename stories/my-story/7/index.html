It Wasn't Magic

It wasn't more than a few weeks after the IRS letter telling me I owed them $983,000 that I had to speak at a seminar.

After everything I had been through, I debated declining the invitation, but I decided to just go, and...

Simply share my story.

Tell that audience exactly what I'd been through, the good, the bad, the ugly, so...

They would never have to go through what I'd just been through.

The moment I stepped off that stage, I got swarmed by people in the audience wanting to give me $997 each for what I offered, and I knew...

I finally understood.

I understood EXACTLY what "HE", the guy in the sharp suit I watched manifest $100,000 in an hour all those years ago, had done.

I thought it was magic. I was SOOO wrong.

As soon as I stepped off that stage, I knew the truth. It wasn't magic.

It was something far more powerful.

He sold well, and he had an audience.

If you have the right audience, you can never go broke.

And that power is in YOU, right now.

Daegan
