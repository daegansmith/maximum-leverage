The $30 Email Ad Vs. 7 Months On The Phone

Subject: Email Porn

Today is email day.

Why?

If success online is about doing the 20% of things that bring 80% of the results, you MUST see how you use your prospects' and customers' inboxes not just as part of the top 20% of where the money is, but...

The top 1%.

Here's what I mean.

Save for a few other things, I could ignore everything else in my business, focus ONLY on email, and STILL have a multimillion-dollar business.

Think about it.

DoubleYourDating.com. A $20 million a year business.

What is it?

Some info products and a bunch of really good emails.

That's why...

When I see all these jokers making such a big fuss about Facebook and blogging and SEO and whatever else, I just don't get it.

None of it is necessary.

What's the one thing you do, and everyone you know does, online?

It's not tweeting.

It's not searching Google.

It's not messing around on Facebook.

And no...

It's not looking at porn.

It's...

EVERYONE checks their email.

Am I right, or am I right?

So, strategically...

If you want to win in your business, if you want to blow out of the gates from the start, where should you focus?

Let me tell you a couple of quick stories...

Back when I had pipe dreams of being a network marketing superstar, I did what anyone would do.

I got a mentor.

His name was Frank Garon. (Good guy. Used to be a truck driver.)

And I followed what he said to do to build a big team.

Which was...

Buy leads and call them.

7 months.

$7,000+ spent on a credit card I had no idea how I was going to pay back.

Hundreds, if not close to 1,000+, hours on the phone.

Hundreds of people slamming the phone down in my ear.

Zero success.

Fast forward six months, after I'd rebuilt my resolve, and...

One little $30 email ad.

Zero hours spent.

14 new people on my team.

Without me there.

Which led to 8,697 people on that team a few years later.

Years after that, every single month, I was running anywhere between 50 and 100 different email ads to bring leads into my business.

Email task #1:

Spend more money on email newsletter advertising, specifically solo ads, to build your list.

Story #2...

That's how I first realized the power of email on the "lead getting" side of things.

Any time you can spend no time on a task and get more results than you would from hundreds or thousands of hours, do whatever that is.

It took me about another year to learn the OTHER side of the coin.

There I was at "The Underground Online Seminar 2" (I think), and this guy named John gets up on stage and talks about how he took his business from $0 to over a million a year in 18 months, and the money magic was in how he used email to pull in sales.

Not passively.

Aggressively.

Two live emails a day.

An earth-shattering epiphany for me.

Up to that point, I had a 10-email autoresponder sequence, but nothing like two live emails a day.

It seemed insane, but so genius.

So...

Intriguing.

When I got home from that event...

What I didn't do was start thinking things like "That's way too much work for me. That's crazy. That can never work."

What I did do was immediately start emailing my list twice a day.

Once in the morning, at 8 a.m., with content.

Once in the afternoon, at 3 p.m., with a short classified ad to send traffic to an offer.

Over the next two months, my business doubled, then tripled.

Remember this for the rest of your marketing life:

"An email a day will keep the J.O.B. away. Two emails a day will make you more than the president of the United States and most heart surgeons."

I've written a LOT of emails since that seminar.

I've made good money.

One month, for the nosy, we were at about $50K about 10 days in, just from writing and sending some emails.

Email task #2:

Email your list twice a day.

Now... maybe you're an affiliate, or you're in a home business right now, and that's OK.

These two new rules of engagement will make you more money there too.

Would you rather have $100 a day or $400 a day for the same effort?

Would you rather make an extra $36,500 over the next year, or an extra $146,000, if...

They both take the same effort?

Get rich, or pick your nose.

Your choice.

To the top,

Daegan
