Belief 1B

The Basics Are Simple, And You're Learning Them Right Now

The objection gets said out loud before anyone can sit with it privately, then answered by handing over the real basics live.

The Five Ground Rules, Then The Room Tries Them

  • Tell it who it is. Give it a job before you give it a question.
  • Give it context: who you are, what you sell, who your customers are.
  • Say exactly what you want back. Ten options, ranked, beats "some ideas."
  • Don't take the first answer. The second and third are almost always better.
  • Use the right model for the job.
  • Then the room does it: the question they asked earlier, asked again with all five rules on the fastest model, then again on a different model.
  • Me too: the same question three ways on screen, asked bare, then with the rules on model one and model two. Nobody has to explain the difference.
  • Round three, for their own business: one line, "Write a Facebook post for my business," and the answer could be anybody's. Same ask, plus "Before you write anything, ask me five questions," and it sounds like them. Then "Before you write it, show me your plan, and tell me what you're assuming," and one correction fixes what it assumed. Same AI, same request. Then the room does theirs. Updated for v13

The Modeling Principle

  • Want to do something you don't know how to do? Find someone who does it well and copy how they do it. They'll watch that happen all night.

Not Rocket Science

  • Did any of that go over your head? Nobody says yes.

The Plant
