Refund Policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

30-day refund

If you're not satisfied with a course you bought from me, you can request a full refund within 30 days of your purchase date, for any reason.

How to request one

Email daegan@maximum-leverage.com from the address you used to buy, or include it in your message, with the word "Refund" in the subject line. You don't need to explain why.

What happens next

  • I'll confirm your request within two business days.
  • The refund goes back to the card or payment method you used. Banks usually take 5 to 10 business days to show it.
  • Your access to the course materials ends once the refund is issued.

After 30 days

Requests made more than 30 days after purchase aren't eligible for a refund.

Questions about a charge

If you see a charge you don't recognize or have a billing problem, please email me first. Most issues can be fixed the same day.
