# Renegade Time Management System
@ My notes from Dan Kennedy's Renegade Time Management course
## Protect Your Time
- Nothing kills time or kills opportunity faster than thoughtlessly agreeing to things on the spot. NEVER do this. It's extremely dangerous.
  - It's caused by immediate, constant access to you. Remove yourself from that situation.
  - Return calls. DO NOT answer them.
- MISTAKE: Saying YES too much.
- People NEVER fight to protect the one thing they can never get back: their time.
- Urgency isn't really all that urgent. Don't let their urgency become yours.
- Do the opposite of what most people do. In most situations, it's the better way to manage your time.
## Control Your Future
- Set goals AND exert control over them. Now, but MORE importantly, into the future.
  - What's the next big win? Leave an open spot for anticipation, like the empty spot on the wall for the next platinum record.
  - Spend more time on what the business and the bank account will look like one year and three years from now.
- If you don't exert control over your future, you're just reacting to life. Most people, me included, are WHOLLY too reactive.
- If you behave in a way that's incongruent with your objectives, you can't achieve them.
- The most predictive indicator of money is time and how you use it.
## Own Your Day
- NEVER start the day by just showing up. That's passive, and others will control your day and your time.
  - Nature abhors a vacuum.
- Use the Positive Power of Negative Preparation. Prepare for what could go wrong in your day and eliminate it BEFORE it can hit you and your productivity.
- Work in a success environment.
- Dan literally scripts his days.
- Use self-imposed deadlines to create self-imposed discipline.
## Schedule First
- Book your schedule out in advance, so it's almost impossible for anyone or anything to interfere with it.
  - On calls, set the start and end time at the outset.
- Why would an appointment with yourself be LESS important than an appointment with the president? It's NOT!
  - Book your own time first. Let them fight over the scraps.
- HUGE: The LESS uncommitted time you have, the LESS distraction and reactivity you let in.
  - FILL YOUR schedule with your shit. Fuck the rest. (Excuse my French.)
- Dan writes almost every single day of his life, and does one thing every day to proactively get more business.
## The Payoff
- EGO: When you finally master your time, others respect the hell out of you, and you feel better than those who have no control over theirs.
