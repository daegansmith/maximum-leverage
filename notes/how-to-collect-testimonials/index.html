How To Collect Testimonials

  • The Moment To Ask
    • Always stay open to the moment a testimonial is there to be had.
      • It's usually a moment when the person is happy.
        • They say "Whoa, thank you."
        • You just finished a call with them.
        • You did something with them or helped them in some way.
        • They gave you a little feedback.
    • Right then, ask: "Hey, do you mind doing a quick case study or writing a testimonial?"
    • Easiest way: write it for them, then ask for their approval.
      • You get exactly what you want, and all they have to do is say yes.
  • The Short Version
    • What was it like before?
    • What was the biggest breakthrough?
    • What's changed since?
  • The Long Version
    • Before
      • What were you looking for before you found me (or the product)?
      • What was going on? What were you trying to achieve?
      • What was working? What wasn't?
      • What missing pieces were you still searching for? The gaps, holes, tools or solutions.
    • Finding Us
      • How exactly did you find us?
        • Always get this. It's great research for the marketing.
      • When you first saw us, what did you think?
      • What made you say yes? What really got you to pull the trigger?
    • Inside
      • Once you got access, or had the experience, what was it like?
      • What didn't you expect?
      • What was better than you expected? What surprised you?
      • If it's about a person: what do you think of them?
        • Their character.
        • Their expertise.
        • Their value to you.
    • After
      • What's different now?
        • Only if things really are different.
      • What would you say to someone who's where you were when you started?
        • Think about yourself back then. What would you tell that person about the process, the choice or the solution?
  • The Tone
    • Personal, earnest, honest.
    • The more sincere a case study or testimonial comes off, the better.
  • Easy Ways To Get More
    • Record it on Zoom and download the video.
    • Trade video testimonials with someone else in your group or mastermind.
    • End by having the person speak about you directly, to you.
    • Testimonials from people you taught carry the most weight, especially when they become an authority themselves. Like me with Russell Brunson.
