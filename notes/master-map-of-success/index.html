The Master Map Of Success

  • Beliefs
    • Problems reflect where we're coming from.
      • At different levels, we run into different problems.
    • Limiting beliefs: all the rationalizations, justifications and excuses for failure.
    • Empowering beliefs: the ones that let us act.
    • HUGE: Every empowering belief becomes a limiting belief as we grow.
    • Beliefs form systems. That's what makes them so hard to change.
    • The problem we all have
      • There are things we want to do, and are capable of doing, that we don't do. It frustrates us. Why?
      • Different parts of us aren't all on board with acting. Get them in agreement and you move.
    • Beliefs don't break. They bend.
      • You can't break someone else's belief, but you can bend it. Wiggle it loose, then change it.
    • One of the most powerful ways to evolve is to deconstruct all of your beliefs.
    • Awareness creates its own momentum. True.
  • How To Change A Belief
    • Name the limiting belief, then split it into internal and external.
      • "I am a failure as a father." Failure is internal. Father is external.
    • Ask what each one means. What does failure mean? What does father mean?
      • Drill down a few levels with questions.
    • Then step outside. See both through another person's eyes.
      • This starts to unhinge the belief.
    • Use this in sales ALL the time.
    • History is written by the victors. Let other people's views define your beliefs, and they're victorious over you.
    • Contemplation is the key to transformation.
  • How Is It Possible To Be An Idiot?
    • Why do we do things? Because they look right, sound right, feel right, or make sense.
    • The "makes sense" people rule the world because of one question: WHY?
      • Sense doesn't make sense.
      • They keep inventing reasons why that don't make any sense.
    • How should you answer a "why" question?
      • Looks right. Feels right. Sounds right.
    • Reasons DON'T change people's minds.
      • To change a mind, change the pictures they see, the feelings inside them, and the voice they use to talk to themselves.
      • The tone of your inner voice drives your emotions. Make it excited and you get excited. Make it sad and you get sad.
    • Two ways to get people to respond: give them something they want, or threaten them with something they don't.
    • We decide and believe based on what feels right, sounds right, looks right and makes sense.
  • Motivation
    • External frame: motivated by what outside influences say.
    • We're moved by carrots (heaven, pleasure) or sticks (hell, pain).
      • Carrot people hate sticks. Use one on them and they get mad and wait for their chance to get even.
    • We all respond to one of three motives.
      • Power: they want control. "When I give you an order, you do it."
        • As a boss: do what I say or hit the highway.
      • Affiliation: they want to be liked. They want harmony.
        • As a boss: wants to be seen as a colleague. People do things for them out of charm.
      • Achievement: they want to get ahead and achieve.
        • As a boss: everything points toward achievement.
    • Toward and away
      • Toward: power moves toward control, affiliation toward being liked, achievement toward the goal.
      • Away: power moves away from anyone trying to control them, affiliation away from being unpopular, achievement away from failure.
    • Approaches to time
      • Some people live in the moment. Others need a schedule.
  • The Secret To Change
    • People do things for their reasons, not yours.
      • If you want people to change, put it in terms of their reasons.
      • With a map of how they operate, you can begin to change them.
    • Ideas separate us. Feelings unite us.
  • Eben's Transformational Thought Process For Information Products
    • Why his clients feel transformed when they finish his programs:
      • He looks at his audience, figures out what the next level is FOR them, then helps them evolve to the next level of THEM.
    • Help people reach the next level and they become customers for life.
      • They can't explain why, but they need more. It can't be just techniques.
    • Focus on the next level of their evolution, refuse to put up with their bullshit to get them there, and they WILL feel it.
      • Don't be afraid to be too extreme. Do it 100% FOR them.
  • How To Transform And Evolve
    • Take the lessons, experiences and memories of the last level with you, but they no longer determine your actions.
    • Getting to the next level is like a video game.
      • It's like Zelda: you get more points for exploring and completing the whole level before moving on.
    • Objectify your demons.
    • Those at higher levels can tell if you haven't evolved. You MUST evolve.
      • You can't fake a higher level to someone who's there. It's like pretending to be an Olympic diver in front of a room full of Olympic divers.
    • Success means something completely different at each level, BUT...
      • Success for the person who thinks "if I could only win the lottery..." is the EXACT same as success at any level. We just don't see it in ourselves.
    • "What have you learned?" really asks, "How will your behavior change?"
      • If your behavior doesn't change, you haven't learned anything.
