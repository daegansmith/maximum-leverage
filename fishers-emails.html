Fishers Workshop Funnel · Emails · Original + Skills, Oct 19

The Email Sequence

Twelve episodes of one story from Jimmy, each one pointing at a free seat on October 27. Then the "You're in" email and the day-of text. Every email in full, as it would send.

What changed on Oct 19. Shorter, about half the length. Numbered as episodes, one through twelve, with no send dates; we set the timing when it's loaded. Every episode ends with a growing "Missed an episode?" list, so anyone who joins late can catch up.

Reordered Oct 19. The emails that hit hardest come earlier: "What's The Catch?" (why it's free) is now third and "$4.58" fourth. New subject lines, written in the style of Daegan's own 2008 to 2010 broadcasts.

Two versions. Owners who opted in but haven't registered get the "not registered" lines, which push to save a seat. Owners who registered get the "registered" lines, which push to show up. Flip the switch to read either.

Red tags are blanks we still need: time, venue, address, parking.

Tell Daegan what's off. Anything that isn't true, doesn't sound like Jimmy, or wouldn't make you show up.
