Why ChatGPT Gave You A Brochure Answer The First Time... And The 2-Word Fix.

Most owners who tried AI walked away thinking it was overrated. That reaction was right. ChatGPT has read more than anyone alive, but it has never once been to your shop, so ask it about your business cold and you get the average answer for a business like yours.

Give it the tour, and it stops sounding like everybody else.

The fix is two words. Tell ChatGPT or Claude about your business the way you'd tell a new hire, then type "Interview me." And the version you tried was a lot weaker than today's.
