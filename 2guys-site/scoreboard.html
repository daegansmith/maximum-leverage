The Scoreboard

Every rotating spot on 2guysandagenie.com shows each visitor one version, and keeps showing them that same one. The scoreboard counts how many people saw each version and how many of them signed up for the night. That tells us which headline, picture and quote actually brings people in.

How To Read It

  • Saw it: people who saw that version. Each person counts once per version.
  • Signed up: of those, how many registered. A sign-up is credited to what they saw on the page they signed up from.
  • Too early to call: fewer than about 10 sign-ups in that spot.
  • Leaning: one version is ahead, not proven.
  • Real: about 95% sure the leader isn't luck.
  • Rates jump around until each version has a few hundred visitors.

Testing It Yourself

Open any page with ?test=1 on the end, like 2guysandagenie.com/?test=1. That browser is marked as a tester from then on: its visits show only under "With test visits," never in the real numbers.

How It's Built

  • The pages: assets/rot.js in the 2guysandagenie repo picks each visitor's versions, remembers them, and sends the counts.
  • The counter: a Cloudflare Worker named 2g-stats at 2g-stats.daegansmith.workers.dev, on the daegansmith@me.com Cloudflare account. Its code is stats/worker.js in the same repo.
  • The numbers: a Cloudflare D1 database named 2g-stats.
  • The scoreboard page: scoreboard/index.html in the 2guysandagenie repo, showing what the Worker serves at /board.

Adding A New Rotating Spot

Give it a short name in the page, and add that name and its versions' plain-English labels to NAMES in stats/worker.js, then redeploy the Worker. Without the label it still counts, under its short code.

Changing The Password

Cloudflare, Workers, 2g-stats, Settings, Variables, KEY. Change it, save, then use "Forget password" on the scoreboard and enter the new one.
