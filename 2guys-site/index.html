Four Things To Try Tonight.

No course. No sign-up. Just your phone.

Do even one, and bring what you found to the next free night.

1Ask who's the best in town

Open ChatGPT and ask: "Who's the best [what you do] in Fishers?"

Are you on the list? Your customers are already asking it that.

Bring your answer Monday. If you're not on it, that's fixable.

2Be your own customer

Three quick tests:

  • Call your own business at 9 p.m.
  • Fill out your own website form.
  • Read your last ten reviews.

What happened? How long until someone got back to you?

Bring what you found. That's where we start.

3Five brave minutes

Tell it about your shop like you would a new hire. Then say two words: "Interview me."

It has read more than anyone alive. It has never once been to your shop. Give it the tour, and it stops sounding like everybody else.

4The five rules for a good wish

  1. Tell it who it is. "You're a marketer with twenty years of experience."
  2. Give it context. Who you are, your business, your customers, your goal.
  3. Say exactly what you want back. "Give me ten, ranked, a sentence on each."
  4. Don't take the first answer. The second and third are almost always better.
  5. Use the right model. Bigger ones think harder.

One rule over all of them: the genie grants, you decide. It drafts. You send.
