The Group-Leader Outreach Plan

How we get local group leaders (chambers, BNI chapters, Rotary, the SBDC, coworking spaces, Facebook group admins) to share Monday's night with their members.

Free AI for Business Night. Monday, Nov 9, 7 to 9pm, Launch Fishers.

The Short Version

  1. A short email first. One question, built to get a reply.
  2. Whoever replies gets the full story, personally, from Jimmy or Paul.
  3. No reply by the next day: the full story anyway, with an apology for the short notice.
  4. Still nothing: Paul calls.
  5. Launch Fishers gets an in-person visit. So does anyone else close enough.
  6. We judge the effort, not a number: everyone reached, everyone followed up.

What The Leader Gets

  • Their members get a free night that leaves them with AI working in their business.
  • The recording and materials from the night.
  • A private session just for their group.
  • A referral share on anyone who becomes a client through them.
  • "Tell us what your members need." Two-way, not just "send us people."
  • We do these every month, so we're a resource they can lean on anytime.

What Members Walk Out With

Bring your phone and your curiosity, and leave transformed: knowing how to use AI, real tasks already off your plate, and a clear plan for what to change next in your business.

Why It Starts Short

Dean Jackson's nine-word email idea. The first message has one job: get a reply.

Short, human, one easy question. "Who should I talk to?" gets answered almost every time, and the leader usually answers "me."

Once they've replied, they're in a conversation and want the details. The full story lands with someone listening, instead of being skimmed by a stranger.

Step 4: Still No Reply

Paul calls.

"Hi, it's Paul Lorkowski. Jimmy and I sent you a note about our free AI night Monday at Launch Fishers. Did it reach you?"

Then he takes it from there.

Who Answers

Humans only. Jimmy or Paul answers every leader personally, by phone or however the leader prefers.

Jimmy's Claude never replies to leaders as Jimmy. It flags each reply to Jimmy and Paul right away, with a suggested next step.

Sending

From Jimmy's personal email, signed 2 Guys and a Genie, in waves, once Jimmy says yes. Facebook and LinkedIn messages and phone calls stay human.

Tracking

  • Each leader gets their own tracked link.
  • The copy button on the leaders page picks up the tag the leader arrived with, so every share traces back to them in GoHighLevel.
  • An optional "How did you hear about us?" field on the registration form.
  • A sign-in sheet at the door, to match sign-ups to who actually showed up. That's what the referral share pays on.

The Leaders Page

It has to match the email: the recording, the private session, the referral share, and "tell us what your members need" go into the perks.

In Person

Launch Fishers

Paul and/or Jimmy visit Rachel Drake in person and ask her to put the night on Launch Fishers' calendar and in the member newsletter. No cold email first.

Everyone else

Wherever time and distance make sense, Paul (or Jimmy) stops by other chambers and groups. Jimmy's Claude maps which ones are close and when they meet this week.

Research For Next Round

Jimmy digs into who the group leaders are: what a chamber director, BNI president or group admin is responsible for, what they're judged on, and what actually moves the needle for them. Then the next round speaks to them from their side.
