How To Read It

Every stage has the same shape, so they can be compared:

  • The wall. What kept going wrong.
  • How many times it was hit. With dates, and what was tried each time.
  • The questions that broke it. The exact questions that changed the answer.
  • What changed. The rule or workflow that came out, with its version.
  • After. What happened on the next piece of work.
  • Still open. How long it has held, and what would prove it wrong.

Entries come from the session record, not from memory. Where the record and memory disagree, the entry says so.

The Lens: Evolution

This is read the way molecular evolution is read (his lens, November 11). The finished system looks designed. The path to it was not. So each entry is a step along the path, in date order, and the record keeps the mess:

  • What got selected. The change that solved the wall and stayed.
  • What drifted through. Rules that neither helped nor hurt and were carried along anyway.
  • What hurt and still passed. Changes that made things worse and survived for a while.
  • What is left over. Parts still in the system from an earlier stage that no longer do a job.

Entries are not sorted into fixes and lessons. They are stages, and a stage can contain all four.
