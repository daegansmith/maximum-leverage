Dr. McCray: The Game Plan

Here's the whole meeting on one picture. Look at it first. The words are underneath, in the same order.

Green is where the money happens. Red dashed is where you'd normally give it away. Every path ends in a card or a call time.

The Five Things To Remember

  1. He's already sold. Don't show him more. Pick up and decide.
  2. Ask first. Write down his words. Hand them back.
  3. Get excited with him about the big picture. That's where he wants to give you money.
  4. Say $2,497. Then stop talking.
  5. Don't stand up without the card on Stripe, or a call time set.

The Words, In Order

Don't read these in the room. Read them twice tonight. Say them your way.

Already Done

He's your doctor and your friend. You pay him every visit.

He's seen 13 sites and 8 videos built from his own practice.

He loved it. Don't redo any of it.

1. Pick Up Where You Left Off

"Doc, you've seen it. Thirteen sites. Eight videos.

I know you liked it.

Today I want us to figure out what we actually do.

Can I ask you a couple of things first?"

2. "Which One Sticks With You?"

Ask one. Then be quiet until he's done. Write down his exact words.

"Of everything you saw, which one sticks with you? Why that one?"

"When a patient sends a friend your way, what do you want that friend to see?"

"Why now?"

3. Hand His Words Back

"Here's what I see.

You've got 234 five-star reviews. People call you Miracle Man. And a stranger landing on your site never sees any of it.

98 percent of your new patients come from people who already love you. You're not short on patients. You're short on a way for them to send you more.

You told me it's so simple people can't believe it works. That's the line."

Then give him back his own words from step 2.

4. What We Build

"Here's what we build. A foundation.

Your website. That's the front door. It tells people who you are in one look.

Your videos. Your patients text them to a friend, and the friend books before they ever call.

Your follow-up. Every patient gets taken care of after they walk out. They come back. They leave the review. They send the friend.

And your dashboard. You see every new patient, where they came from, and what's working. That's where we find the next dollar.

Whatever system you have to use for your patients, it stays. We build around it and connect what we can."

If he says "Isn't it already done?"

"The mockups are the picture. What you're paying for is it working. Live, hooked to your booking, follow-up running, and me keeping it right."

5. My Story Marketing

"Doc, I developed a process called My Story Marketing.

It changes the way people see you.

I want it in your site at the deepest level.

And I've got great marketing people behind me helping me do it.

One thing I'd love from you. Once you love what this does, let me tell your story. You'd be my first chiropractor.

And I'll need an hour of you on camera. Your words, your face. That's what makes it yours."

6. The Big Picture

This is where you get excited with him. Let it run.

"Doc, can I tell you what I really see?

Have you ever thought about your own book?"

If he says "People don't read books anymore."

"That's not what I mean. Your own audiobook.

Something your patients tap into, and tell their friends to tap into.

You don't have to name anybody. Just, 'I take care of the best athletes in the world. Everybody's just a person. Here's the truth about your back, after I've seen everybody. And here's how your life gets better.'

Picture people walking out of here posting a video. 'This is the guy. His audiobook changed my life.'

And come on, Doc. You take care of Colts guys. Let's get them saying it, on camera. On your site. I'll set it up for you.

That's the level I want for you. Because that's the level of testimonial I want from you."

Before any of it goes public

"What are you allowed to say about the Colts? We'll build to exactly that."

7. Value First

"If you went to an agency, a chiropractor's site alone runs nine to twenty-five thousand. One to two months to build. Then you're on their platform, looking like everybody else, waiting a week for a change.

I'm right down the road. You text me, it's done.

You know what a new patient's worth to you. What's that number?"

Let him say it.

"What if every one of them brought you one more? That's what the video and the follow-up do."

8. The Fair Exchange

"Doc, here's how I see it.

I come in, you fix me, I pay you. Every time. Because you're amazing at what you do.

This is what I'm amazing at.

So I'm going to hook you up. But it's got to be a real thing, or neither of us values it.

You don't hand somebody a free cheesesteak after an adjustment."

9. $2,497. Then Silence.

"At the workshop on the 27th, what I'm doing for you, I'm charging five to ten thousand down. Plus five hundred to a thousand a month.

You're my guy. You get the best deal anybody gets.

$2,497 for the whole setup. Everything.

After that, you just cover the hard costs to keep it running. About a hundred bucks a month. Or put it on your own servers and pay nothing.

Do you want me hosting it, or your own?"

Then stop. The next person who talks is him.

After He Says Yes

"Need something fixed? Text me. I see you all the time.

Something breaks at midnight, I'm there. Because it's you. Anybody else, I'm rolling over and going back to bed.

And anything bigger, the audiobook, the Colts testimonials, we'll figure out a fair price when we get there."

Take The Card

"I'll run it right now on my Stripe. What card do you want to use?

You'll get a confirmation right away.

Then an email that spells out exactly what happens next."

Then celebrate. Out loud.

"Doc, you're going to love this. Who's the first person you're sending the video to?"

If He Hesitates

"Can I just pay you in adjustments?"

"Nah, Doc. I pay you, you pay me. That's how we both keep valuing it."

"Let me think."

"Of course. What's the part you're thinking about?"

If it's still open, set the time to talk before you stand up.

"I need to ask my wife."

"Smart. Show her the showcase tonight. I'll call you tomorrow at four. That work?"

Every Path Ends In One Of These

The card on Stripe. Or a call time set.

Never "let me know."

You're going to love this.

Jimmy
