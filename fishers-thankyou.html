The Bread Company That Thought It Was Failing

Jim makes bread his customers write him letters about. That's how much they love it.

But at the last workshop, he sat in front of the whole room and said it straight: "I need to get to profitability. Right now, I'm not making any money at it."

So we asked him a few questions. Live. What it costs to make. What he charges. What it costs to ship.

He was losing $4.58 on every box he shipped.

Here's why. Shipping costs him about the same whether a box holds three loaves or six. And everything he sold came in threes.

I looked at him and said, "Jim, you don't have a bread problem. You have a box problem."

That's a fix. But it wasn't the big find that night.

The big find was his story.
