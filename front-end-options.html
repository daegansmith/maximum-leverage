How They Fit Together

  • The Assessment is built and pulls worried owners, so it's ready to test first.
  • The Driveway pulls the curious crowd with money, and owners, through the story.
  • We Already Built Yours pulls owners through pride, and it uses work you're already planning to do.
  • Nominate pulls the community, and turns customers into the people who bring owners in.

All four can end in the same place: the workshop.
